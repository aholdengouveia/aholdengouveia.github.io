\subsection*{Privacy Policy Analysis}

You are tasked with analyzing the privacy policies of a popular social media platform.  Pick one of the following options: 
        \begin{itemize}
            \item Facebook: Link: \href{https://www.facebook.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.facebook.com/}}}
            \item TikTok: Link: \href{https://www.tiktok.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.tiktok.com/}}}
            \item Instagram: Link: \href{https://www.instagram.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.instagram.com/}}}
            \item WhatsApp: Link: \href{https://www.whatsapp.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.whatsapp.com/}}}
            \item Snapchat: Link: \href{https://www.snapchat.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.snapchat.com/}}}
            \item Pinterest: Link: \href{https://www.pinterest.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.pinterest.com/}}}
            \item LinkedIn: Link: \href{https://www.linkedin.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.linkedin.com/}}}
    \end{itemize}
        
        

The links above go to each platform's home page, not its policies. Finding the privacy policy and Terms of Service is part of this lab. They are usually linked at the bottom of the page or in the account settings. Many platforms have more than one policy, such as a cookie policy or a separate policy for the EU. Use any of them that help you answer the questions.

Read the policies carefully and answer the following questions.

\textbf{Answer in your own words.} Do not copy and paste from the policies. Instead, back up your answers with screenshots.

\textbf{Screenshots:} every answer that comes from the privacy policy or Terms of Service (questions 3 to 14) must include a screenshot of the section(s) that back up what you are saying.

Each screenshot should have your name, term, and year.  One of the easiest ways to do that is make a text document with your name and term/year on your computer and save it to use all term. Any screenshots that don't include this information won't be counted.

\subsection*{Questions}
            \begin{enumerate}
                \item Which social media platform did you pick, and why?
                \item Finding the policies
                \begin{enumerate}
                    \item What is the link to the platform's privacy policy? If there is more than one, include all links.
                    \item What is the link to the platform's Terms of Service?
                    \item Did you use any other policies, such as a cookie policy or an EU policy? If yes, include their links.
                \end{enumerate}
                \item What types of data are collected from users?  Make a list.
                \item How is the collected data used by the platform?  Make a list or table.
                \item Third parties
                \begin{enumerate}
                    \item Is user data shared with third parties?
                    \item If so, for what purposes?
                \end{enumerate}
                \item What rights do users have regarding their personal data?
                \item Legal options. These are often in the Terms of Service rather than the privacy policy.
                \begin{enumerate}
                    \item If there is an issue, what are the options for litigation?
                    \item What steps are detailed in the policies that cover legal recourse for bad practices?
                \end{enumerate}
                \item Legal options in other countries. Look for a section or separate policy for the EU, the EEA, or GDPR.
                \begin{enumerate}
                    \item Would the options for litigation change if you were an EU citizen?
                    \item What about a citizen of another country outside the USA or EU?
                \end{enumerate}
                \item Children
                \begin{enumerate}
                    \item What age does the platform consider a \textit{child}?
                    \item Does that change how their data is handled?
                \end{enumerate}
                \item What measures does the platform take to ensure the security of user data?
                \item What are the options for users to control their privacy settings?
                \item Cookies and tracking
                \begin{enumerate}
                    \item Does the platform use cookies or tracking technologies?
                    \item If yes, what kinds, and can users opt out?
                \end{enumerate}
                \item What are the data retention policies of the platform?
                \item How does the platform handle user consent and opt-in/opt-out mechanisms?
        \end{enumerate}
