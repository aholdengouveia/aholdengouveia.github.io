\section*{How to Pick a Topic}
Pick one topic from this list for your final project. The full instructions are in the Final Project document, linked under Resources.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Topics are first come, first served. Claim yours by starting a thread in the Final Project discussion on Brightspace, as the instructions explain.
    \item Two students can't present the same topic. If someone has already claimed yours, pick another one.
    \item You may pick a topic that isn't on this list, but check with me first.
    \item Cover your topic in a balanced, non-biased way, and focus on the data.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Topics}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Music recommendations:} how Spotify and other apps pick your next song, and how Spotify Wrapped is made
    \item \textbf{The TikTok For You page:} how short-video apps decide what you see next
    \item \textbf{YouTube recommendations:} how YouTube uses watch time and clicks to recommend videos
    \item \textbf{Dating app matching:} what data dating apps use to decide who you see
    \item \textbf{Ride share and delivery pricing:} how apps like Uber and DoorDash use demand data to set prices and pay
    \item \textbf{Ticket pricing:} how concert and event tickets use dynamic pricing, and what data drives it
    \item \textbf{Sneaker and resale markets:} how resale sites like StockX track prices and sales data
    \item \textbf{Shopping recommendations:} how ``customers also bought'' and ``you might like'' recommendations work
    \item \textbf{Fake reviews:} how sites try to spot fake reviews in ratings for products, restaurants, and apps
    \item \textbf{Influencer analytics:} how engagement rates are measured, and how fake followers are found
    \item \textbf{Sports analytics:} how teams use player tracking data for performance, strategy, and injury prevention
    \item \textbf{Sports betting odds:} how sportsbooks use data to set odds and lines
    \item \textbf{Fantasy sports:} how fantasy sports projections and rankings are made
    \item \textbf{Video game analytics:} how game studios use player data to balance games and design new content
    \item \textbf{Esports statistics:} how esports teams use data to scout players and plan strategy
    \item \textbf{Fitness trackers and smartwatches:} what step, sleep, and heart rate data can and can't tell you
    \item \textbf{Running and cycling apps:} how apps like Strava make heat maps and leaderboards from route data
    \item \textbf{Training data for AI image generators:} where the images come from, and how the data shapes what the AI makes
    \item \textbf{Training data for AI chatbots:} what text chatbots learn from, and why the data matters
    \item \textbf{Deepfake detection:} how data is used to train tools that spot fake images and videos
    \item \textbf{Streaming shows and movies:} how Netflix and other services use viewing data to decide what to make and cancel
    \item \textbf{Music charts:} how Billboard and other charts count streams, sales, and radio plays
    \item \textbf{Credit scores:} what data goes into a credit score, and how it's calculated
    \item \textbf{Budgeting apps:} what personal finance apps do with your transaction data
    \item \textbf{AI resume screening:} how companies use data and AI to sort job applications
    \item \textbf{College admissions data:} how colleges use data in admissions and financial aid
    \item \textbf{Predicting student success:} how schools use data to find students who might need extra help
    \item \textbf{Language learning apps:} how apps like Duolingo use learner data to plan lessons and reminders
    \item \textbf{A/B testing:} how apps and websites test two versions of something to see which works better
    \item \textbf{Self-driving cars:} how self-driving cars collect and use sensor data
    \item \textbf{Wildlife and conservation data:} how sensors, cameras, and tags are used to track animals and protect habitats
    \item \textbf{Wastewater testing:} how testing sewage is used to track illnesses in a community
    \item \textbf{Space data:} how telescopes and satellites collect data, and how NASA shares it with the public
    \item \textbf{Crowdsourced data:} how Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, and citizen science projects collect data from volunteers
    \item \textbf{Data journalism:} how journalists use data to find and tell stories
    \item \textbf{Polling and election forecasting:} how polls are collected and weighted, and how forecasts are made
    \item \textbf{The census:} how the US Census counts people, and how the data is used
    \item \textbf{Careers in data:} what data analysts, data scientists, and data engineers do, and how to get started
    \item \textbf{Retail stocking:} how stores decide what to stock, and where, using sales data
    \item \textbf{Subscription cancellations:} how subscription companies predict who's about to cancel, and what they do about it
\end{enumerate}
