\subsection*{Personal Data}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Go to Link: \href{https://aboutmyinfo.org/identity}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://aboutmyinfo.org/identity}}} and see how unique your data is.
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item How unique are you?
            \item Are you easy to identify?
            \item Take a screenshot of your result.
        \end{enumerate}
        \item Other information about you
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item What other information do you think might be available about you?
            \item Try Googling yourself. What pops up? Give some examples.
        \end{enumerate}
        \item Go to Link: \href{https://haveibeenpwned.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://haveibeenpwned.com/}}} and check an email address or phone number.
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Have you been pwned?
            \item If yes, in which breaches?
            \item Take a screenshot of your result. Blur out any personal information you do not wish to share.
        \end{enumerate}
        \item Go to Link: \href{https://robinlinus.github.io/socialmedia-leak/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://robinlinus.github.io/socialmedia-leak/}}}
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item What social media are you logged in to?
            \item Did you realize you were logged in to each of them?
            \item Do you know how to log out of each?
        \end{enumerate}
        \item Go to Link: \href{https://webkay.robinlinus.com/}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://webkay.robinlinus.com/}}}
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item What did it show about your browser?
            \item Did you realize how much information you were sharing?
            \item Was any of it surprising? If yes, which ones?
        \end{enumerate}
    \end{enumerate}
