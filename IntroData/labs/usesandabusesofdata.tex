\section*{Objectives:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The goal of this lab assignment is for students to distinguish between good and bad UI/UX using real-world examples. Through practical examples and observations, students will gain an understanding of how to evaluate UI/UX through a checklist of what to look for.
    \item Students will also find and evaluate a recent real-world example of a bad or unethical use of data.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Answer the following questions}
Each of the 4 websites above showcases some of the best and the worst of UI/UX. Going through the entries/comments/winners/submissions, answer the following questions using the 10 point checklist and feedback pointers from Link: \href{https://designlab.com/blog/design-critique-checklist-for-ux-designers#ten}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://designlab.com/blog/design-critique-checklist-for-ux-designers\#ten}}} If the point isn't relevant to your question, you may include an explanation of why instead. For example, if you want to critique a physical product, it may not have a navigation or mobile design to give feedback on, and it's ok to explain that.

You should pick 4 specific examples from the above websites.  The 4 examples are going to be your favorite, least favorite, most humorous, and which entry should have won. Your favorite and least favorite can come from any of the 4 websites. Your most humorous must come from the bad examples, and the one that should have won must come from one of the 2 award websites (the Webby Awards or the UX Design Awards).

For each example, fill out the 10 point checklist (10ptChecklist), linked here as a Word document Link: \href{https://www.aholdengouveia.name/IntroData/labs/10ptChecklist.docx}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.aholdengouveia.name/IntroData/labs/10ptChecklist.docx}}} or as an OpenDocument file Link: \href{https://www.aholdengouveia.name/IntroData/labs/10ptChecklist.odt}{\textbf{\nolinkurl{https://www.aholdengouveia.name/IntroData/labs/10ptChecklist.odt}}}

    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Your favorite design
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Name it and include a link and a screenshot of it.
            \item Why is it your favorite?
            \item Fill out the 10 point checklist for it.
        \end{enumerate}
        \item Your least favorite or most aggravating design
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Name it and include a link and a screenshot of it.
            \item Why is it your least favorite?
            \item Fill out the 10 point checklist for it.
        \end{enumerate}
        \item The bad example you found most humorous
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Name it and include a link and a screenshot of it.
            \item Why did you find it the most humorous?
            \item Fill out the 10 point checklist for it.
        \end{enumerate}
        \item From one of the 2 award websites (the Webby Awards or the UX Design Awards), a submission that didn't win that you think should have won an award
        \begin{enumerate}
            \item Name it and include a link and a screenshot of it.
            \item Why do you think it should have won?
            \item Fill out the 10 point checklist for it.
        \end{enumerate}
    \end{enumerate}

\section*{Bad Use of Data Article}
Find an article from the past year about a use of data that you consider bad or unethical. The article must be about a specific company or industry, and it must come from a reputable source. Use the CRAAP test to check that it is reputable.

Include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The citation and link for the article
    \item A short summary of the article
    \item Why you consider this use of data bad or unethical
    \item Your CRAAP test results for the article
\end{enumerate}
