How to Pick a Topic

Pick one topic from this list for your final project. The full instructions are in the Final Project document, linked under Resources.

  • Topics are first come, first served. Claim yours by starting a thread in the Final Project discussion on Brightspace, as the instructions explain.
  • Two students can't present the same topic. If someone has already claimed yours, pick another one.
  • You may pick a topic that isn't on this list, but check with me first.
  • Cover your topic in a balanced, non-biased way, and focus on the data.

Topics

  1. Music recommendations: how Spotify and other apps pick your next song, and how Spotify Wrapped is made
  2. The TikTok For You page: how short-video apps decide what you see next
  3. YouTube recommendations: how YouTube uses watch time and clicks to recommend videos
  4. Dating app matching: what data dating apps use to decide who you see
  5. Ride share and delivery pricing: how apps like Uber and DoorDash use demand data to set prices and pay
  6. Ticket pricing: how concert and event tickets use dynamic pricing, and what data drives it
  7. Sneaker and resale markets: how resale sites like StockX track prices and sales data
  8. Shopping recommendations: how “customers also bought” and “you might like” recommendations work
  9. Fake reviews: how sites try to spot fake reviews in ratings for products, restaurants, and apps
  10. Influencer analytics: how engagement rates are measured, and how fake followers are found
  11. Sports analytics: how teams use player tracking data for performance, strategy, and injury prevention
  12. Sports betting odds: how sportsbooks use data to set odds and lines
  13. Fantasy sports: how fantasy sports projections and rankings are made
  14. Video game analytics: how game studios use player data to balance games and design new content
  15. Esports statistics: how esports teams use data to scout players and plan strategy
  16. Fitness trackers and smartwatches: what step, sleep, and heart rate data can and can't tell you
  17. Running and cycling apps: how apps like Strava make heat maps and leaderboards from route data
  18. Training data for AI image generators: where the images come from, and how the data shapes what the AI makes
  19. Training data for AI chatbots: what text chatbots learn from, and why the data matters
  20. Deepfake detection: how data is used to train tools that spot fake images and videos
  21. Streaming shows and movies: how Netflix and other services use viewing data to decide what to make and cancel
  22. Music charts: how Billboard and other charts count streams, sales, and radio plays
  23. Credit scores: what data goes into a credit score, and how it's calculated
  24. Budgeting apps: what personal finance apps do with your transaction data
  25. AI resume screening: how companies use data and AI to sort job applications
  26. College admissions data: how colleges use data in admissions and financial aid
  27. Predicting student success: how schools use data to find students who might need extra help
  28. Language learning apps: how apps like Duolingo use learner data to plan lessons and reminders
  29. A/B testing: how apps and websites test two versions of something to see which works better
  30. Self-driving cars: how self-driving cars collect and use sensor data
  31. Wildlife and conservation data: how sensors, cameras, and tags are used to track animals and protect habitats
  32. Wastewater testing: how testing sewage is used to track illnesses in a community
  33. Space data: how telescopes and satellites collect data, and how NASA shares it with the public
  34. Crowdsourced data: how Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, and citizen science projects collect data from volunteers
  35. Data journalism: how journalists use data to find and tell stories
  36. Polling and election forecasting: how polls are collected and weighted, and how forecasts are made
  37. The census: how the US Census counts people, and how the data is used
  38. Careers in data: what data analysts, data scientists, and data engineers do, and how to get started
  39. Retail stocking: how stores decide what to stock, and where, using sales data
  40. Subscription cancellations: how subscription companies predict who's about to cancel, and what they do about it
