Web Security

Learning outcomes:

  • Describe common website attacks, such as SQL injection and defacing
  • Explain how websites, and the people using them, can be protected from attacks
  • Explain how HTTPS and certificates work, and what happens when a certificate authority is compromised
  • Compare the benefits and risks of single sign-on and VPNs
