Physical Security

Learning outcomes:

  • Explain why physical access is dangerous, and why physical security is often overlooked
  • Identify physical threats to systems, such as servers, cables, backups, and peripheral devices
  • Describe ways to protect systems, such as secure boot, signed updates, and allowlisting
  • Describe security risks from Internet of Things (IoT) devices at home and at work
