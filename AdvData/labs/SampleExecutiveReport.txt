SAMPLE EXECUTIVE REPORT
=======================
This shows the FORMAT to follow, using my book data as the EXAMPLE ONLY.
Your report must be about YOUR own company and data.

An executive report is short and focused. Leaders read it to make a
decision, so it has a clear purpose, uses real numbers, explains them
in plain words, and ends with one action they can take.


To:       Leadership team
From:     Your Name, data team
Date:     2026-04-28
Re:       Request DATA-7720, end-of-term data report


PURPOSE
This term, the data team built the store's first inventory database.
This report covers what the data shows, the problems we found and
fixed, and the one thing we need from you next.

KEY FINDINGS
- Classics make up half our catalogue: 50 of our 99 books.
- Customers rate Fantasy highest. Fantasy books average 4.34 stars,
  compared with 4.05 for Classics, but we only stock 12 Fantasy books.
- 71 of our 99 books are rated 4 stars or higher.

CHART
[Insert your chart from the Data Visualization lab here.]
Alt text: Bar chart of books by genre. Classics is by far the biggest
group, with 50 of 99 books, about three times as many as Fiction, the
next biggest.

DATA QUALITY
- 24 books had a fake placeholder ISBN. These are now marked as
  unknown, so searches by ISBN give correct results.
- 31 books have no price. These are left out of price calculations,
  so averages aren't pulled down by missing values.
- 6 books are digital formats with no shelf section. We've asked the
  manager whether to stock print copies.

NORTH STAR METRIC
Our North Star is books sold per week, since sales are how the store
makes money. We can't measure it yet, because sales aren't in the
database. The closest measure we have today is the share of books
rated 4 stars or higher: 71 of 99, or 72%.

ACTION ITEM
Approve collecting sales data from the till starting next month,
about 300 sales a day (see request DATA-2058). This lets us track our
North Star every week, and see whether stocking more Fantasy books
pays off.


TIPS
- Lead with why the reader should care. Save how you did it for your
  other documents.
- Every finding should have a number, and say what the number means.
- One clear action item is better than five vague ones.
- Keep it to about one page.
