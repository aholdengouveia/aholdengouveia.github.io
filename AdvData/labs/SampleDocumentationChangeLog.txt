SAMPLE DOCUMENTATION CHANGE LOG
===============================
This shows the FORMAT to follow, using my book data as the EXAMPLE ONLY.

A change log goes near the start of your documentation, so anyone can
see what changed, when, and who to ask about it. The newest entry goes
at the top.

Your change log starts with just ONE entry, version 1.0. The other
entries below show what it looks like after a few updates over the term.

How to number versions:
  - Small change, such as fixing a note or adding a query: 1.0 to 1.1
  - Big change, such as adding or removing a table: 1.1 to 2.0

TIPS
- "What changed" should be specific enough that someone could find the
  change. "Updated the documentation" doesn't help anyone.
- If you changed something because someone asked, say who. It explains
  why the change was made.
- Never delete old entries. The history is the point.
